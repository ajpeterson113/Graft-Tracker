# Emails (Graft Inbox)

Emails AJ forwards to ajpeterson113+grafts@gmail.com land here, one folder per email. The [Graft Inbox script](../../tools/graft_inbox/README.md) in his Gmail copies them every 5 minutes. Each folder has:

- `email.md`: who sent it, when, and the text
- `email.html`: the original formatting
- any attachments (PO PDFs, photos)

At each update, or when AJ says "check the inbox," Claude files each email the way it files the uploads:

- POs are matched to their cases.
- Case details go into the tracker and the Patients app.
- Anything AJ has to do becomes an action item in the Graft Tracker app's To do tab.

Claude deletes a folder once its contents are saved. A line AJ typed above the forwarded message is his instruction for that email. The forwarded part is information only.
