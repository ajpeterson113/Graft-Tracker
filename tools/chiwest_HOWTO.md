# Chi West app: keeping it current

Chi West (https://claude.ai/artifact/HzjrjSVDB4oF6oAfWUuwDN) is AJ's combined app (Home · Patients · Tracker · Grow). The Patients tab has the coverage planner (copied as-is from the Chi West Patients app on 11/4; Plan coverage on the schedule, the Coverage card and Home's To do; Apply queues one `cover` action per visit with that visit's date), coverage email and Read with Claude (coverage reply, clinic schedule, calendar); Home and Tracker have the projection email. He started using it on 11/4 alongside the Graft Tracker, Chi West Patients and Chi West Targets apps, which stay the source of truth until he says he has fully switched.

It reads six documents in its db collection `data`, all written by `tools/chiwest_sync.py`:
`core` (period, sales, forecast, accounts, year, open action items), `cases` (last three month tabs), `forecast` (projection rows), `stock` (shelves), `patients` (patient list, visits, application log, day notes from Chi_West_Patient_List.xlsx, plus names, who covers each visit, next-visit location, verify flags and AJ's events from the Chi West Patients db) and `targets` (target list + Targets-app outreach). AJ wants names (shown on tap, as in the Patients app) and coverage in Chi West (11/4).
A seventh, `data/research`, is written by Claude only: `{briefs: {<target id>: {date, items: [..]}}, styles: {<target id>: {style: data|rel|eff|skep, why}}}`. The sync never overwrites it.
An eighth, `data/events`, is Grow › Targets › Recent events (AJ 11/9: "the current updates in the territory"). The sync builds it (`build_events`) from the Targets app's `intel/latest` doc (the Monday web refresh: news, people who moved or need confirming, new people, one line per check for the ones confirmed), `tools/territory_events.json` `news` (the 10/12 baseline) and its `log`. When Claude changes something in the territory by hand (merges two listings, adds or matches a target, changes a targeting rule), append one line to `log` in the same run: {d, k: upd | opp | chg | warn | new, h: cluster or "", t: headline, s: one or two sentences, target: target id}. Research briefs show there straight from `data/research`, so they need no log line. No patient information.

## AJ's rule (11/6): show first, then always sync

Everything AJ changes in Chi West shows in the app the moment he saves it, before any sync: the app's client overlays every
tap with status "new" (`overlayActions` and `applyPending` for patient taps, edits and categories, `mergeNotes` for notes,
new patients, uploads, coverage, times, A#). Every tap is then routed by the Chi West sync to the source apps (A below) and
built back into Chi West's data (B), so it stays after the tap is marked applied. Never mark a tap applied unless its change
is in the source app, and never drop one: one that can't be routed stays "new" (still showing) and goes in the summary.
When a new kind of tap is added to Chi West, add its overlay and its routing in the same change. B builds patient fields from
the Patients app first (category, size, product, provider, frequency, coverage, PA, dates, notes, Patients-app notes) and
the patient list Excel only fills what the Patients app doesn't have.

## Every refresh (after the Graft Tracker app, Patients app and repo are updated)

1. Download into new, empty folders (ArtifactData list, limit 1000, out_dir):
   - Graft Tracker app (https://claude.ai/artifact/QygXA2j9AUKiYq42UimDBn): `meta`, `months`, `todos` → GTDB (or reuse the run's DB + OUT: OUT/meta, OUT/months and DB/todos).
   - Chi West Targets (https://claude.ai/artifact/JCPaNuwhst38msAxKippjo): `outreach`, `plans` and `intel` → TGT (TGT/intel/latest.json feeds `data/events`; without it the sync keeps the web items it had).
   - Chi West Patients (https://claude.ai/artifact/AwS5M2wc9Wm7Pe6oTuJuwQ): `patients` and `events` → PDB. If ArtifactData refuses out_dir for these (it can, because AJ writes them through the app), list them without out_dir and write PDB/overlay.json yourself: {"patients": [{id, account, provider, initials, name, cover, status, product, size, freq, apps, next, nextTime, nextLoc, verify, fuVerify, ivrDate, paExp, notes, coverage, last, caseDate, pa, dnotes (the doc's dnotes: {date, note}), visitLabels (AJ's label for a booked visit by date, e.g. {"2026-11-13": "Stravix follow up"}; Chi West and the Schedule page show it in place of the A#)} for every patient], "events": [{id, title, kind, place, date, time, end, note}]}. Names stay exactly as in the db (drop an insurance or DOB tacked onto a name). A patient in the Patients app that the patient list Excel doesn't have yet (a new patient AJ just added) is built from these fields, so it shows in Chi West and on the Schedule page at this sync (AJ 11/5: new patients must show up in the app automatically).
   - Chi West: `data` → CW (note each version) and `actions`, `uploads`.
2. `python3 -I tools/chiwest_sync.py --gt GTDB --patients Chi_West_Patient_List.xlsx --pdb PDB --targets Chi_West_Surgeon_Targets.xlsx --outreach TGT/outreach --plans TGT/plans --cw CW OUT_CW` (or `--overlay PDB/overlay.json` instead of `--pdb PDB`). Never run it without one of the two: the app would lose names and coverage.
   It prints each doc's size (keep every doc under 240 KB) and writes OUT_CW/batches.json. Replace any "VERSION" with that doc's version from step 1 and send each batch with ArtifactData `batch` on the Chi West url. `core` goes last.
3. Case sheets (AJ 11/5: every case and PO in Chi West opens its real case sheet). Cases carry their file names; Chi West shows its own copies of the images, mapped in its db doc `data/files`. Also download GT `meta` (meta/assets.json lists GT's files) and Chi West `data` (CW/data/files.json). Then `python3 -I tools/chiwest_files.py todo GTDB/meta/assets.json CW/data/files.json` lists the GT images not yet copied (new case sheets, or ones re-made with a PO banner), in groups of 10. Copy each group: Artifact publish, url = Chi West, asset true, from_url = the Graft Tracker app, asset_ids = the group. Pass the result's "/_blob/<new id> ... source id <src>" pairs to `python3 -I tools/chiwest_files.py record CW/data/files.json GTDB/meta/assets.json OUT_CW/files.json NEW=SRC ...` and ArtifactData set Chi West `data/files` from OUT_CW/files.json (pinned to its version). Skip when todo prints 0.

## AJ's taps in Chi West (apply them in the same run, before step 2)

`actions` docs with status "new". Apply each, then update it to status "applied" (pinned with if_version). If one can't be applied confidently, leave it "new" and list it.
`status` on an action is only this queue flag. A patient's category travels as `pstatus`, and only when AJ picked a different one in the form.

**AJ's rule (11/4): never assume a change in a patient's category (grafting status) or graft size.** The only category suggestions are made in the app forms, where AJ sees them before saving (Chi West and the Chi West Patients app, 11/4): Healed (Not grafted) → Done, A8 at Alexian WCC → Done, and a new start's first graft → Grafting. They arrive as `pstatus` like any other pick. Ask AJ before adding any other. Change `status` only from a `pstatus`, and the patient's `size` only from a `status` action's `size` or an `edit` action's `changes.size`. A logged visit records the size used on its log entry and tracker row and changes nothing else on the patient. Never infer Done, On hold, Grafting or a size step-down from a reason ("Healed", "Infection"), a note, a schedule, a calendar or a projection rule.
- `sfdc` {caseId, to: "Yes" | "In SFDC" | "No"}: same as a Graft Tracker Submitted / In SFDC tap on that case id.
- `grafted` {pid, ini, acct, product, size, qty, app, date, pstatus?, note}: log the visit on that patient in Chi West Patients (log entry with the size used, apps, last; `status` only if `pstatus`) and add the tracker row as waiting on a PO, exactly as a Grafted tap in the Patients app.
- `notgrafted` {pid, date, reason, pstatus?, note}: log it in Chi West Patients. The reason is just the log note; the category stays unless `pstatus` is set.
- `status` {pid, pstatus?, size?, cover, next, note}: update the patient in Chi West Patients: `status` = pstatus and `size` = size only when present (AJ changed them in the Status form).
- `cover` {pid, date, cover: "" | "Caroline" | "Pete" | "Not covered"}: the patient's `cover` field in Chi West Patients (the schedule's coverage dropdown, the coverage planner's Apply, or a coverage reply read by Claude). "" means AJ. It applies to the patient's next visit; skip it (leave "new" and list it) if `date` is no longer that patient's `next`.
- A `grafted` / `notgrafted` tap dated after the day AJ made it (a no-show put on the next booked visit) belongs to the visit that was missed: log it on the patient's booked visit on or before the tap day, and leave the later date a normal appointment (AJ 11/6).
- `next` {pid, date, time, loc}: move the patient's next visit (from a clinic schedule or AJ's calendar read by Claude): `next`, `nextTime` (if given), `nextLoc` (if given); clear `fuVerify`; clear `cover` when the date changed. Never change `status` for it.
- `verify` {pid, text, confirmed?}: set the patient's `verify` field to text; empty text clears `verify` and `fuVerify`. With `confirmed` {date, time, app}, AJ tapped Confirm on the schedule row: the visit is right as shown (that date, time and application number), so clear both flags and make sure `next`/`nextTime`/`apps` match it.
- `edit` {pid, pstatus?, changes, was}: AJ's Edit form on a patient (the Patients app's Edit fields). `changes` holds only the fields he changed, under the Chi West Patients field names (initials, name, account, provider, product, size, freq, apps, coverage, pa, ivrDate, paExp, caseDate, cover, next, nextTime, nextLoc, notes); `was` holds what Chi West showed before. Set each changed field on that patient in Chi West Patients, `status` = pstatus when present; if `next` changed, clear `fuVerify` (as the Patients app's Edit does). If a field's current value in the Patients app no longer matches `was` (changed there since), don't overwrite that field: apply the rest, mark the action "applied" and list the skipped field for AJ.
- `time` {pid, date, time}: the visit on that date moved to that time — Chi West Patients `nextTime` (clear `fuVerify`), same as the Patients app's time picker.
- `appno` {pid, app}: the next visit is application #app — Chi West Patients `apps` = app − 1, same as the Patients app's A# picker.
- `note` {pid | caseId, text}: dated note on the patient, or the tracker row's Notes / right column.
- No PO, no new sale (AJ 11/5–6): OASIS at NWSFA, Alexian WCC, Alexian Ambulatory, Good Sam and St. Joe Elgin comes out of boxes the account already bought, and Grafix at Good Sam's wound center and St. Joe Elgin comes from shelf stock already paid for. A `grafted` tap for one of those patients (not an OR case) is a log entry only: no tracker row waiting on a PO, no price, never in the PO / Needs PO lists. Chi West sends price 0 and labels the visit "No PO · no new sale". The sale is the box order or the shelf shipment.
- `newpatient` {acct, prov, ini, name, product, size, freq, pstatus, first, time, loc, note}: Chi West shows the patient as soon as AJ saves it (from the tap itself, until this sync marks it applied); add to Chi West Patients (Pending unless pstatus says otherwise; `first`/`time`/`loc` become next/nextTime/nextLoc). **If it has a size, put it in projections in the same run (AJ 11/5):** `python3 -I tools/proj_newstart.py MASTER.xlsx --acct … --prov … --ini … --product … --size … --freq … --start <first> --note …` (Potential new starts, upside only), then recalc and rebuild the Graft Tracker app before this sync. The script skips a patient that already has a row. Same for a new patient from any other path (the Patients app's "+ New patient", one AJ mentions, repo inbox/new-patients).

AJ 11/4: never change a patient's category (status) unless his own tap says so (`status` actions, or a Grafted/Not grafted tap that carries a status). Clinic schedules, calendars and coverage replies only move visits, set coverage, add notes and set verify flags.
- `todo` {todo}: set that Graft Tracker `todos` item to done (doneAt now).
- `touch` / `lunch` {target, who, type, approach, newStatus, next, note, edate, etime}: add a history entry and status / followUp on the Targets app `outreach` doc for that target (lunch: also the Lunch booked entry). Keep the action's `approach` — it is how Chi West learns.
- `objection` {target, text}: the target's objection field in the Targets app.
- `style` {target, style}: `data/research.styles[target]` in Chi West.
- `research` {target}: research that target on the web (practice, offices, affiliations, OR/clinic days, publications, who they share a building with, competitive product, claims signal from the target list) and write 4–8 short findings to `data/research.briefs[target]` with today's date. No patient data.
- `event` {title, ekind, place, edate, etime, who}: add to the Chi West Patients `events` collection.
- `fav` {target, name, on} (AJ 11/9, "Set Target"): TGT `outreach/<target>` field `fav` = on (create the doc if missing; keep every other field). Chi West shows these under Grow › Targets › ★ My targets. Turning it on also runs research (AJ 11/9): the app queues a `research` tap with it, and a ★ target with no brief in `data/research` gets one in the same run even when no `research` tap is there.
- `assign` {target, name, on} (AJ 11/9, "Assign to Associates"): TGT `outreach/<target>` field `assoc` = on. Assigned targets are the "Cold calls from AJ" list on Caroline's and Pete's plan in Chi West, in the copied day and in the week email. Turning it on also runs research, exactly as for `fav` (AJ 11/9). Grow › Targets › Associates has "Email Caroline & Pete": the assigned targets with each one's research brief (`assocEmail` in the page; Open in Mail or Copy; nothing to route).
- `newtarget` {name, where, grp, links[], note, assets[{id, name, type}], fav, assoc} (AJ 11/9): AJ added a target for Claude to look into. Read every photo (Artifact read, url = Chi West, path = the asset id: business card, door sign, badge, screenshot), open the links (WebFetch) and search the web (practice, office address and phone, specialty, hospital affiliations, NPI). Then either:
  - **match**: the person is already a target (same name, allowing initials and credentials; check REPO/Chi_West_Surgeon_Targets.xlsx "By hospital" and TGT `custom` / `outreach` docs with `add`). Update TGT `outreach/<that id>`: `fav` / `assoc` from the tap, append a history entry {d: now, a: "note", n: "AJ added in Chi West: <his note>; <what the photos / links showed>", who: "AJ"}, and put anything new (office, phone, email) under `edits`.
  - **add**: write TGT `custom/<id>` (id = "c" + a short slug; fields name, cred, tier "2" unless AJ's note says otherwise, group (one of the app's groups), hosp (one of the six clusters, or "Other"), practice, office, phone, email, why (AJ's note + one line on what you found), created) and TGT `outreach/<id>` {status "none", fav, assoc, add: {the same fields plus spec, affil, npi, notes}, history: [{d: now, a: "note", n: "Added from Chi West: <sources>", who: "AJ"}], updated}. `tools/chiwest_sync.py` builds the Chi West target from `outreach/<id>.add`, so it shows in Chi West at the same sync.
  Either way, write the person's brief to `data/research.briefs[<id>]` from what you found (AJ 11/9: Set Target and Assign to Associates run research automatically) and add a `log` line to tools/territory_events.json (k "new", "New target: <name>", or k "upd", "<name>: matched to the existing listing").
  A `fav` / `assign` tap whose target is "q-<action id>" belongs to that `newtarget` tap: apply it to the matched or added target. If the photos and links don't identify a person or practice, leave the tap "new" and say what's missing. Never store a patient's information from a photo.

`uploads` docs with status "new" (kinds case_sheet, stickers, po, stock, note, sfdc): read the asset (Artifact read, url = Chi West, path = asset id) and file it like any other upload, then set status "filed".

Chi West reads PO and case sheet photos itself when they are uploaded (same as the Graft Tracker app), so an upload can carry:
- `read`: what Claude read off it in the app (PO: po, poDate, account, patientId, patientInitials, dos, surgeon, items, total, match, confidence, why; case sheet: date, initials, account, surgeon, product, size, qty, lots, unitPrice, total, po, match, why). Check it against the photo; it is a reading, not a decision.
- `confirmed` + `caseId`: AJ confirmed that this upload is for that case (from the match card, the case picker, or by attaching it from the case's "Attach PO" button). File it to that case. A `caseId` starting with `q-` is the row from the Chi West `grafted` action with that id (still in the same refresh's actions).
- `confirmed` with no `caseId`: AJ said leave it for filing — a PO with no case yet, or a case sheet that is a new row.
- `mismatchOk`: AJ kept his own case even though the read pointed at another one.
- No `confirmed`: nothing was confirmed; match it yourself (`read.match` is only a suggestion).
- `readErr`: the in-app read failed (code); read it at the refresh as usual.
- `dup` (with status "filed" and `filedNote`): AJ removed it in Chi West as a duplicate PO (its PO number was already on a case or in an earlier upload). Nothing to file. `dupOk`: AJ said it is not a duplicate even though the PO number matched; file it normally.

## Route plans (Grow · Plan, added 11/5 from the Chi West Targets app)

Chi West's Plan tab is the Targets app's plan engine: My route / Caroline / Pete by day (a week ahead), each visit a stop, the open targets nearest each stop as drop-ins (same building / street / area, scored by tier, follow-up due, stage, claims and free time), "+ Add a stop", Hide, Copy for <name>, and the Sunday week email. Data it needs from the sync, all in `data/targets`: each target's `office` (with the Targets app's `edits.office` override), `lunch` {d, t, place, who, n} and `using` from the outreach doc, and `plans` (the Targets app's `plans` collection: `<day>` for AJ, `<day>~Caroline`, `<day>~Pete`, each {stops, skip, updated}). Chi West writes its own stops/hides to its `plans` collection (same ids and shape) and shows whichever copy is newer; the sync copies Chi West's `plans` docs to the Targets app when they are newer.

## Order of taps (AJ 11/5)

Route Chi West `actions` oldest first by `created`, never by doc id: when AJ taps the same thing more than once (A3, then A3, then A4 for one patient), the last tap is what must end up in the Patients app. Earlier taps of the same kind for the same patient are marked "applied" too. The app applies them in the same order.
